## 1. Core Rule: Never Bake Essential UI Text into Raster Graphics

A critical conversion rule for all KRAK & CO. campaign assets:
> **All headlines, pricing, discount badges, and Call-to-Action buttons MUST be rendered as live HTML/CSS text.**

Visual background graphics, sunburst rays, and character illustrations provide emotional framing, while the text remains crawlable, translatable, responsive, and sharp on Retina displays.

## 3. Responsive Cropping & Focal Point Strategy

- **Hero Banner:**
  - Desktop: 2-column layout. Left 55% is reserved as clean negative space for HTML headline, subhead, and dual buttons; Right 45% houses the retro character and snack bowls.
  - Mobile: Graphic transitions to a compact header arrangement, with character and snack photography positioned above the live CTA buttons.
- **Regional Banners:**
  - Standardized `16:9` aspect ratio on desktop (`600×340px`) and `16:10` on mobile (`360×220px`).
  - Text is positioned on top of subtle dark gradient scrims (`rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`) to maintain > 7:1 contrast on all screens.
