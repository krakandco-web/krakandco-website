## 1. Persona Methodology & Framework

The customer personas for KRAK & CO. are developed using qualitative behavioral archetypes based on shopping intent, regional culinary attachment, basket composition, and friction tolerance. 

Rather than relying on generic demographic stereotypes, these profiles model **jobs-to-be-done (JTBD)**, cognitive biases, device preferences, risk thresholds, and repeat-purchase triggers across the Indian snack market.

### Persona A: The Everyday Snack Buyer ("Chai-Time Sridhar")
- **Profile:** 32-year-old software professional, lives in an urban apartment, works hybrid. Drinks 2 cups of tea daily at 11 AM and 5 PM and requires a crunchy companion.
- **Shopping Motivation:** Replace bland supermarket biscuits with freshly fried, artisanal Indian savouries.
- **Purchase Intent:** High frequency, medium basket size (₹350 – ₹600).
- **Main Expectations:** Fresh crunch that lasts (no stale or soggy chips), clean ingredients, transparent oil usage, prompt 3-day delivery.
- **Product Discovery Behaviour:** Browses category pages (Mixtures, Crisps, Murukku) or picks Bestsellers from the homepage.
- **Likely Objections:** *"Will these snacks stay crispy after opening?"*, *"Is shipping going to cost as much as the snacks?"*
- **Purchase Barriers:** Expensive delivery fees on orders under ₹300; slow shipping timelines.
- **Trust Requirements:** "Freshness Locked" nitrogen packaging badge, clear expiry dates (90-day shelf life).
- **Preferred Device:** Mobile browser (65%) during work breaks; laptop (35%) while working.
- **Website Entry Point:** Homepage Bestsellers or "Mixtures" category.
- **Key Conversion Features:** 
  - Dynamic Free Shipping Progress Bar (`"Add ₹120 more for Free Shipping"`).
  - 1-Click "Add to Cart" directly on product cards.
  - Multi-pack size toggle (`250g` default).
- **Repeat Purchase Opportunity:** Bi-weekly replenishment subscription prompt or 1-tap WhatsApp reorder.

### Persona B: The Nostalgic Regional Snack Lover ("Hometown Meenakshi")
- **Profile:** 40-year-old educator who moved from Palakkad (Kerala) to Gurugram (NCR). Longs for authentic snacks prepared the traditional way.
- **Shopping Motivation:** Reconnecting with regional culinary roots and childhood memories through food.
- **Purchase Intent:** High intent, regional specificity.
- **Main Expectations:** 100% authentic ingredients (e.g. Nendran banana chips fried strictly in pure cold-pressed coconut oil, not palm or cottonseed oil).
- **Product Discovery Behaviour:** Heads straight to the **South Indian Classics** regional collection; scrutinizes the ingredient list and oil type.
- **Likely Objections:** *"Is this genuine Kerala style or a generic commercial imitation?"*
- **Purchase Barriers:** Lack of ingredient clarity; absence of regional cultural cues.
- **Trust Requirements:** Prominent "Pure Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil" and "Traditional Recipe" seals; regional heritage storytelling.
- **Preferred Device:** Mobile smartphone (80%).
- **Website Entry Point:** `/collections/south-indian` via search ("authentic kerala banana chips online").
- **Key Conversion Features:**
  - Regional Hero Banner with cultural visual motifs (Gopuram, Kathakali, temple architecture).
  - Explicit ingredient & oil disclosure on Product Detail Pages.
  - "Pairs With Filter" (Tea-Time, Festive, Temple style).
- **Repeat Purchase Opportunity:** High loyalty; will reorder monthly and share with regional community WhatsApp groups.

### Persona C: The Family Household Shopper ("Host Rajesh")
- **Profile:** 46-year-old family head in Bengaluru. Buys groceries and household treats for children, parents, and visiting relatives.
- **Shopping Motivation:** Stocking the pantry with a variety of snacks that satisfy diverse generational tastes (mild for kids/grandparents, spicy for adults).
- **Purchase Intent:** High volume, high AOV (₹750 – ₹1,500).
- **Main Expectations:** Value for money, freshness, variety of spice levels, multi-pack savings.
- **Product Discovery Behaviour:** Navigates via "Combos & Bundles" or explores both South Indian and North Indian collections to build a diverse basket.
- **Likely Objections:** *"Are these snacks too spicy for my kids or elderly parents?"*, *"Do they offer larger family packs?"*
- **Purchase Barriers:** Lack of spice indicators; inability to buy larger 500g economy sizes.
- **Trust Requirements:** Explicit spice ratings (`Mild 🌶️`, `Medium 🌶️🌶️`, `Spicy 🌶️🌶️🌶️`), 100% Vegetarian badge, FSSAI licensing assurance.
- **Preferred Device:** Tablet or Desktop (60%) during evening family browsing.
- **Website Entry Point:** Homepage Regional Banners or `/collections/combos`.
- **Key Conversion Features:**
  - Multi-pack selector (`100g`, `250g`, `500g Family Pack — Save 10%`).
  - Family Combos (`South-North Sampler Box`).
  - Clear spice meter on every card.
- **Repeat Purchase Opportunity:** Monthly pantry refill reminder; "Reorder Entire Basket" button in account history.

### Persona D: The Young Explorer & Snacking Foodie ("Trend-Seeker Ananya")
- **Profile:** 24-year-old digital marketer in Mumbai. Loves discovering aesthetic indie food brands on Instagram and snacking during OTT streaming sessions.
- **Shopping Motivation:** Delighted by retro pop-art visual branding, quirky packaging, and novel crunch textures.
- **Purchase Intent:** Moderate intent, exploratory impulse buyer (₹250 – ₹500).
- **Main Expectations:** Engaging visual identity, unboxing delight, clean label snacks with no MSG.
- **Product Discovery Behaviour:** Lands from social media onto product detail pages or search results; scans visual galleries and reviews.
- **Likely Objections:** *"Is this brand genuinely good, or just good marketing?"*
- **Purchase Barriers:** Clunky checkout, forced account registration, lack of social proof.
- **Trust Requirements:** Verified customer photos on the Reviews Wall, user-generated content (UGC), 4.8★ rating metrics.
- **Preferred Device:** Mobile smartphone (95% iOS/Android).
- **Website Entry Point:** Direct PDP or Instagram ad landing.
- **Key Conversion Features:**
  - Instant UPI checkout (GPay / PhonePe).
  - High-res photo galleries with macro crunch details.
  - Social proof review badges (`"Loved by 25,000+ Snackers"`).
- **Repeat Purchase Opportunity:** Re-engages through seasonal product drops and festive limited-edition packs.

### Persona E: The Festive & Corporate Gifter ("Gifting Head Vikram")
- **Profile:** 38-year-old corporate operations manager or festive family gifter. Needs 10 to 50 premium gift hampers for Diwali, New Year, or corporate appreciation.
- **Shopping Motivation:** Premium presentation, assorted regional specialties, hassle-free bulk ordering, reliable dispatch dates.
- **Purchase Intent:** High ticket value (₹1,500 – ₹10,000+).
- **Main Expectations:** Elegant packaging (tin boxes / matchbox-style retro gift boxes), assorted sweets and savouries, customizable greeting notes.
- **Product Discovery Behaviour:** Clicks "Gift Boxes" on the main navigation or hero banner.
- **Likely Objections:** *"Will the boxes arrive crushed or delayed?"*, *"Can I send to multiple addresses?"*
- **Purchase Barriers:** Lack of visual preview of hamper contents; absence of corporate GST invoice option.
- **Trust Requirements:** Dedicated Corporate Inquiries channel, GST billing option at checkout, delivery guarantee dates.
- **Preferred Device:** Desktop (70%) for corporate orders; mobile (30%) for personal festive gifts.
- **Website Entry Point:** `/collections/gift-boxes`.
- **Key Conversion Features:**
  - "What's Inside The Box" visual breakdown with itemized pill thumbnails.
  - Add Custom Gift Message toggle during checkout.
  - Dedicated "Inquire Corporate / Bulk Orders" WhatsApp trigger.
- **Repeat Purchase Opportunity:** Annual festive gifting cycle; bi-annual corporate gifting contracts.

### Persona F: The Habitual Repeat Muncher ("Loyal Customer Deepak")
- **Profile:** 29-year-old remote analyst who has already ordered twice from KRAK & CO. Knows exactly what he likes: 2 packs of Madurai Mixture and 1 pack of Kerala Banana Chips.
- **Shopping Motivation:** Zero-friction replenishment; needs snacks in the kitchen within 3 days.
- **Purchase Intent:** Maximum conversion intent.
- **Main Expectations:** Instant checkout in under 30 seconds using saved address and preferred payment method.
- **Product Discovery Behaviour:** Skips discovery entirely; goes to Search, Account, or uses the Cart.
- **Likely Objections:** *"Why do I have to re-enter my address or card details?"*
- **Purchase Barriers:** Slow page loads, expired sessions, missing reorder shortcut.
- **Trust Requirements:** High existing trust; expects consistent product taste and crunch.
- **Preferred Device:** Mobile (90%).
- **Website Entry Point:** Direct URL, SMS/WhatsApp order update link, or Account page.
- **Key Conversion Features:**
  - "1-Click Reorder" in Account Order History.
  - Saved Address Selector in Checkout.
  - Default payment preference (Pre-selected UPI).
- **Repeat Purchase Opportunity:** Continuous bi-monthly recurring orders.
