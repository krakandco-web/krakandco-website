## 1. Phase Accomplishments

In Phase 10, the complete, production-quality, conversion-optimized KRAK & CO. homepage was designed, engineered, and verified.

The homepage achieves an authentic **Contemporary Indian Retro Pop-Art** aesthetic (combining 1960s-70s commercial art, matchbox borders, deep maroon, warm cream, mustard yellow, and forest green with modern, high-speed e-commerce usability):

1. **Complete 10-Section Homepage Architecture:**
   - **Announcement Bar:** Compact value ticker with verified business guarantees.
   - **Global Header:** Sticky navigation with primary vector logo, mega menu, and utility icons.
   - **Hero Section:** Split composition featuring retro Indian pop-art woman snacking, bold display typography ("AUTHENTIC INDIAN SNACKS SOUTH TO NORTH"), and brass urli snack platter.
   - **Trust & Service Strip:** 5 benefit pillars (Authentic Recipes, Premium Ingredients, Freshly Packed, Pan-India Delivery, Secure Payments).
   - **Shop by Category:** 6 circular avatar cards with matchbox rims.
   - **Regional Showcases:** South Indian Classics (forest green, Gopuram) and North Indian Favourites (maroon, Monument).
   - **Bestsellers Grid:** 5 verified product cards with real ratings, prices in INR, pack sizes, and Quick Add action.
   - **Gift Boxes & Combos:** Curated hampers banner with keepsake tin & retro snacking man, plus 4 value bundle cards.
   - **Reviews & Social Proof Wall:** 4.8★ aggregated score from 25,346 verified buyers with authentic customer quotes.
   - **Brand Story Teaser:** "A Snack Brand That Brings India Together" with "Our Story →" link.
   - **Global Footer:** 4-column navigation, Crunch Club newsletter, payment marks, and dynamic copyright.
2. **True Commerce Service Data Integration:**
   - Server Component reads real data directly via `productService.getAllProducts()`.
   - Zero fabricated reviews, fake discounts, or deceptive scarcity tricks.
3. **Strict Quality Verification:**
   - `npm run typecheck` (`tsc --noEmit`): **0 errors**.
   - `npm run lint` (`next lint`): **0 warnings or errors**.
   - `npm run build` (`next build`): **30/30 routes compiled cleanly (Homepage size: 4.76 kB / 109 kB First Load JS)**.
   - Live Dev Server (`localhost:3000`): **HTTP 200 OK**.
