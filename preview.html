THE CLASSIC CRUNCH.

THENKULZAL MURUKKU

★★★★★ (4.9 • 420 reviews)
₹199
A golden, delicate, traditional spiral that brings the familiar joy of Indian snacking to every bite.

Some classics never need an upgrade. Just a really good crunch.

Meet Thenkulzal Murukku. Delicate spirals, golden crunch and the comforting flavour of a snack that never goes out of style.

Whether it's alongside your chai, on the snack table or straight out of the pack, this is your everyday crunch done right.

Taste Traditional
Texture Light & crunchy
Pack Size 150 g
✨Authentic Recipe
🌱Pure Ingredients
🚚Pan-India Fresh Delivery

Rooted in South India's culinary heartlands, our recipe preserves time-honoured technique — hand-extruded spirals fried in small batches to preserve an unmistakable, melt-in-mouth airy crunch.

Rice Flour, Urad Dal Flour, Cold Pressed Groundnut Oil, Cumin Seeds, Asafoetida (Hing), Sesame Seeds, Himalayan Pink Salt. No artificial colours, zero preservatives.

Best enjoyed within 90 days from manufacture. Store in a cool, dry place. Reseal tightly after opening to keep that signature KRAK crunch intact.
