## 1. Illustration Creative Philosophy

The illustration language of KRAK & CO. draws inspiration from the golden era of **Indian commercial lithography, 1960s matchbox graphics, hand-painted cinema signage, and classic biscuit tin packaging**.

It adds warmth, humor, and cultural pride to the shopping experience while celebrating India's rich culinary rituals.

## 2. Core Illustration Categories

### 2.1 Hero Brand Characters
- **The Saree Connoisseur:** A chic, retro Indian woman wearing a traditional silk saree with retro cat-eye sunglasses, blissfully enjoying crispy banana chips. Embodies the union of timeless regional heritage and youthful confidence.
- **The Aviator Uncle:** A distinguished gentleman with a classic handlebar mustache and retro aviator sunglasses savoring tea-time mixture beside a vintage snack hamper.

### 2.2 Regional Architectural Motifs
- **South Indian Motifs:** Classical Dravidian temple gopurams, coconut palm fronds, Kathakali crown silhouettes, brass filter coffee tumblers, traditional uruli vessels.
- **North Indian Motifs:** Mughal & Rajasthani scalloped palace arches, India Gate silhouettes, brass chai kettles, intricate jali lattice patterns.

### 2.3 Decorative Framing & Matchbox Borders
- Concentric thin-and-thick rule borders.
- Scalloped edge dividers.
- Starbursts, sun rays, and hand-drawn sparkle accents (`✦ ✧ ✦`).

### 2.4 Empty-State & System Illustrations
- **Empty Snack Bowl:** Hand-drawn ceramic bowl with flying crumbs and friendly text (*"Whoops! Your snack bowl is empty"*).
- **Munched 404 Page:** Retro illustrated character holding an empty matchbox with playful bite marks.

## 3. Style Execution Rules & Boundaries

1. **Snack Photographs vs. Illustrations:** 
   - **Product Cards MUST use real snack photography.** Never use illustrated cartoons to depict product contents on catalog cards or product detail pages.
   - Illustrations are strictly reserved for **section backgrounds, brand storytelling, category framing, and empty states**.
2. **Culturally Respectful & Authentic:** Depict regional traditions with accuracy and warmth; never use exaggerated caricatures or insensitive tropes.
3. **No Decorative Noise Over Text:** Illustrations must never compete with or reduce the legibility of product prices, ingredient labels, or checkout forms.
