## 2. Category Detail & Cultural Context

### 2.1 South Indian Heritage Collection (`public/assets/illustrations/south-indian/`)
- **Gopuram & Palm Silhouette (`illustration-gopuram.svg`):** Honors the architectural majesty of Madurai, Thanjavur, and Palakkad. Uses forest green gradient with warm mustard filigree.
- **Visual Role:** Anchors the South Indian Classics banner and regional collection header.

### 2.2 North Indian Heritage Collection (`public/assets/illustrations/north-indian/`)
- **Royal Palace & Scalloped Arch (`illustration-monument.svg`):** Honors the regal culinary heritage of Delhi, Rajasthan, and Punjab. Uses deep crimson with scalloped Mughal battlements.
- **Visual Role:** Anchors the North Indian Favourites banner and regional collection header.

### 2.3 Decorative Elements & Motifs (`public/assets/illustrations/decorative/`)
- **Maroon Sunburst (`motif-sunburst-maroon.svg`):** Radiating retro rays with scattered gold crunch stars. Placed behind the hero typography and character artwork.

### 2.4 System & Feedback Illustrations (`public/assets/illustrations/general/`)
- **Empty Cart Bowl (`empty-cart-bowl.svg`):** Ceramic bowl with flying crumbs, creating an endearing, friendly recovery prompt.
- **Munched 404 Matchbox (`illustration-404-munched.svg`):** Matchbox with bite marks, diffusing error frustration with brand humor.

## 3. Strict Boundary Rules
- **No Fictional Food Illustrations on Cards:** Never replace actual product photos with cartoon drawings on commercial cards.
- **No Inaccurate Regional Stereotypes:** Ensure architectural and culinary depictions are authentic, culturally accurate, and respectful.
