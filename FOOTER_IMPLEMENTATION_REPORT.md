## 1. Footer Section Breakdown

The KRAK & CO. global footer provides trust reinforcement, comprehensive catalog links, customer support access, and subscriber acquisition:

### 1.1 Top Value & Trust Bar
Four high-contrast value pillars:
1. **Authentic Recipes:** "Heritage recipes, zero shortcuts" (Sparkles icon).
2. **Cold-Pressed Oils:** "100% Pure Coconut & Ghee" (Coconut icon).
3. **Nitro Fresh Packed:** "90-day crunch lock guarantee" (ShieldCheck icon).
4. **Pan-India Delivery:** "Free delivery above ₹499" (India flag icon).

### 1.2 Main 4-Column Navigation Layout
- **Column 1: Brand & Culinary Heritage:** Logo, mission statement, 100% Vegetarian badge, and FSSAI food certification stamp.
- **Column 2: Shop Snacks:** Direct links to Shop All, South Indian, North Indian, Bestsellers, Gift Hampers, and Combos.
- **Column 3: Customer Support:** Track Your Order, FAQs, Contact Support, Shipping SLA, Taste & Freshness Guarantee, Cancellation.
- **Column 4: The Crunch Club (Newsletter):** 10% off promotion with email subscription input and instant development feedback banner.

### 1.3 Bottom Legal & Payment Ribbon
- Legal links: About Krak & Co., Reviews, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions.
- Genuine Payment methods: UPI (GPay/PhonePe), RuPay, Visa/Mastercard, Netbanking.
- Dynamic copyright year: Generated via `new Date().getFullYear()`.
