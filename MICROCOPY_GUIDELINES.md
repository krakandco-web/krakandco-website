**Date:** November 2026

---

## 1. Brand Tone & Voice Framework

The voice of KRAK & CO. is like an enthusiastic, foodie friend who knows the best street-corner snack makers across India.

### 1.1 The Voice Quadrants
- **Playful & Nostalgic:** Celebrates childhood tea-time rituals, train journey snacks, and festive family banter.
- **Culturally Rooted & Honest:** Proud of authentic regional techniques (coconut oil frying, hand-coiled murukku, stone-ground podi).
- **Bold & Contemporary:** Fresh, punchy, and confident; avoids archaic or stuffy food terminology.
- **Crystal Clear in Commerce:** **Absolute zero ambiguity** when it comes to money, ingredients, allergen warnings, and shipping timelines.
