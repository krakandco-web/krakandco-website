## 1. Brand Identity & Personality

KRAK & CO. is a premium direct-to-consumer Indian snack house celebrating the extraordinary culinary diversity of India's savoury and sweet heritage.

### 1.1 Brand Personality Attributes
- **Playful:** Irreverent wit, celebratory crunch sounds (*KRRRAK!*), vibrant packaging, and joyous snacking moments.
- **Bold:** Unapologetic Indian flavours (pungent curry leaves, freshly ground black pepper, fiery red chilies, pure roasted hing).
- **Youthful:** Designed for a contemporary generation who value clean labels, zero palm oil, and high-energy aesthetics.
- **Nostalgic:** Recalls childhood train journeys with paper cones of roasted peanuts, grandmother's brass murukku presses, and festive sweet boxes.
- **Culturally Rooted:** Authentic regional recipes honoring Palakkad coconut oil frying, Madurai mixture blending, and Bikaneri sev mastery.
- **Contemporary & Accessible:** Seamless digital shopping experience, clear typography, instant mobile UPI payments, and reliable pan-India delivery.

## 2. Creative Direction: Contemporary Indian Retro Pop-Art

The visual identity fuses **1960s–70s Indian matchbox graphics, hand-drawn commercial lithography, and retro roadside signage** with **the speed, clarity, and precision of tier-1 e-commerce UI**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE KRAK & CO. RATIO                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┤
│  80% CONTEMPORARY E-COMMERCE UX        │  20% RETRO BRAND EXPRESSION   │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
│  • Clean 4-column product grids        │  • Matchbox architectural arches│
│  • High-contrast pricing & badges      │  • Sunburst halftone backdrops │
│  • Standard cart & checkout steppers   │  • Hand-drawn retro characters │
│  • 100% accessible Poppins body text   │  • Vintage stamp trust seals   │
│  • Frictionless 44px tap targets       │  • Warm nostalgic color palette│
└────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 The "Do Not Cross" Creative Boundaries
- **NOT a chaotic vintage poster museum:** The site must never sacrifice text legibility, navigation speed, or conversion clarity for decorative clutter.
- **NOT an outdated traditional food directory:** Avoid faux-parchment backgrounds that look dusty or unhygienic. The aesthetic is warm, crisp, appetising, and fresh.
- **NOT generic corporate minimalism:** Avoid cold monochrome grids, sterile grey cards, or faceless sans-serif layouts that lack Indian soul and cultural warmth.

---

## 3. Brand Tone in Visual Communication

1. **Snack Photography is King:** Crispy food photography takes center stage. Macro close-ups of golden banana chip edges, spiral murukku ridges, and fine sev threads provide undeniable proof of crunch.
2. **Retro Illustrations as Emotional Accents:** Hand-drawn character illustrations (e.g. the retro lady in a saree with sunglasses, the mustachioed aviator uncle) appear at key emotional touchpoints (Hero banner, Gift boxes, About story, 404 page).
3. **Architectural Framings:** Inspired by classical Indian matchbox labels and biscuit tins, cards and category banners feature subtle arch headers, scalloped dividers, and multi-line border rules.
