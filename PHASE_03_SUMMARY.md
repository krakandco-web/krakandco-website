## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 03 established the comprehensive conversion rate optimization (CRO) strategy, behavioral customer psychology profiles, section-by-section homepage blueprint, 16 priority low-fidelity wireframes, and component behavioral requirements for KRAK & CO.

All specifications translate the brand identity and the approved visual reference artboard into conversion-focused, accessible, and commercially viable storefront architectures.

---

## 2. Key Deliverables & Decisions

1. **Six Behavioral Personas Defined:** Everyday Muncher, Nostalgic Regional Lover, Family Household Shopper, Young Explorer, Festive Gifter, and Habitual Repeat Muncher.
2. **10-Stage Conversion Funnel:** Mapped from initial awareness to post-purchase retention with granular cognitive friction analysis and solutions.
3. **Behavioral Psychology Framework:** Applied Hick's Law, Fitts's Law, Cognitive Fluency, Ethical Loss Aversion, and Social Proof without resorting to deceptive dark patterns.
4. **Homepage Blueprint (13 Sections):** Detailed section-by-section specification matching the approved artboard.
5. **Low-Fidelity Wireframes:** Full desktop and mobile wireframes covering all 16 priority storefront surfaces.
6. **Component Conversion Strategy:** Product card interaction states, CTA color and sizing hierarchy, slide-over cart drawer free-shipping engine, and live search overlay.
7. **Brand Tone & Microcopy Standards:** Crisp, friendly, culturally rooted brand voice prioritizing total transparency in money, ingredients, allergens, and delivery.
8. **Telemetry & Experimentation Blueprint:** GA4 e-commerce event specification and high-leverage A/B testing roadmap.
