## 2. Studio Photography Direction & Setup Guide

### 2.1 Lighting & Color Balance
- **Key Light:** 45-degree angle natural daylight (5200K–5600K) diffused through a 1.2m softbox to accentuate the golden frying edges.
- **Fill Light:** White reflector at 90 degrees to maintain gentle shadow definition.
- **Accentuating Oil Sheen:** Crisp specular reflections on banana chips and sev threads confirming authentic coconut/groundnut oil frying.

### 2.2 Backgrounds & Framing
- **Background Surface:** Clean, uniform Warm Biscuit Cream (`#FAF3E0`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Contact Shadow:** Soft natural contact shadow (`rgba(38, 35, 33, 0.08)`) grounding the bowl.
- **Aspect Ratio:** Master square `1:1` (`1200×1200px`), exported to WebP/AVIF (`400×400px` for listing cards).

## 3. Development Fallback Strategy

Until actual high-resolution studio photographs are supplied by the business:
- All 12 SKUs are mapped to the high-fidelity SVG visual placeholders in `public/assets/placeholders/`.
- The architecture guarantees zero layout shift or broken image links when real photographs are dropped into `public/assets/products/`.
- No fake or hallucinated third-party brand packaging will be used.
