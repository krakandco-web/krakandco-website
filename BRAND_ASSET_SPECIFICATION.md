## 2. Logo Usage Standards & Rules

### 2.1 Clear Space
- A minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter `"K"` in the logo must surround the logotype on all four sides.
- No text, icons, decorative borders, or photographic elements may encroach into this buffer zone.

### 2.2 Minimum Dimensions
- **Digital Screen:** Minimum height `36px` on desktop; `28px` on mobile screens.
- **Symbol / Favicon:** Minimum dimension `16×16px`.

### 2.3 Background Compatibility
- **On Light Surfaces (`#FFF8EE`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F3E4C5`):** Use the standard **Deep Maroon (`#7A1C1C`)** logo with Mustard (`#E6AC2A`) stroke and Ink Black drop shadow.
- **On Dark Surfaces (`#262321`, `#581111`):** Use the **Inverted Logo** where the letterfill transitions to Cream (`#FFF8EE`) and the drop shadow remains high-contrast.

### 2.4 Prohibited Logo Modifications (Strict Brand Governance)
1. **DO NOT** stretch, condense, or distort the logo aspect ratio.
2. **DO NOT** alter the approved color combination (e.g. turning the logo blue or purple).
3. **DO NOT** add realistic photorealistic bevels, 3D extrusions, or glowing neon drop shadows.
4. **DO NOT** remove the `& CO.` subscript or separate the crunch star.
