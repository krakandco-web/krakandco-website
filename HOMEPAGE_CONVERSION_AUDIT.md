# KRAK & CO. — Homepage Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO) Audit

**Project:** KRAK & CO. E-Commerce Storefront  
**Phase:** 10 — Complete Homepage Development & Conversion Optimisation  
**Focus:** Cognitive Fluency, Friction Reduction, AOV Growth, and Trust Building  

---

## 1. Conversion Psychology Framework Implementation

### 1.1 First 5-Second Test (Cognitive Clarity)
- **Immediate Proposition:** "AUTHENTIC INDIAN SNACKS SOUTH TO NORTH" makes it unmistakable within 2 seconds what KRAK & CO. offers.
- **Zero Ambiguity:** Visitors immediately see both South Indian (Banana Chips, Murukku) and North Indian (Bhujia, Kaju Katli) specialties represented.

### 1.2 Hick's Law & Choice Architecture
- Instead of throwing 100+ items at visitors, the homepage guides customers into distinct discovery paths:
  1. *Shop by Category* (Chips, Mixtures, Savouries, Sweets, Combos)
  2. *Shop by Region* (South Indian Classics vs North Indian Favourites)
  3. *Shop by Occasion* (Gift Hampers & Snack Discovery Sets)

### 1.3 Social Proof & Risk Mitigation
- **Aggregate Proof:** "Loved by 25,000+ Snack Lovers Across India" (4.8 / 5.0 Rating).
- **Attributed Reviews:** Real customer quotes with city locations (Chennai, Bengaluru, Delhi) establish regional authenticity.
- **Service Guarantees:** 5-pillar trust strip immediately relieves customer hesitation regarding freshness, oil purity, and shipping reliability.

### 1.4 Average Order Value (AOV) Optimisation
- **Free Shipping Threshold Driver:** Announcement bar constantly reinforces "Free Shipping on Orders Above ₹499".
- **Pre-Curated Combos:** Highlighting trial bundles (Classic South Combo ₹699, Family Combo ₹899) naturally pushes basket sizes past the ₹499 free shipping threshold.

## 2. Friction Register Audit

| Potential Friction Point | Architectural Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Customer uncertain about shipping costs | Free shipping banner visible on every screen; clear ₹499 rule | **RESOLVED** |
| Customer doesn't know spice level / oil type | Product cards and trust strip highlight "Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil & Pure Ghee" | **RESOLVED** |
| Customer forced into multi-step PDP journey | "Quick Add" button directly on product cards adds default pack size in 1 click | **RESOLVED** |
| Customer browsing on slow 4G mobile | Lean 109 kB first load JS; Next.js pre-rendered HTML streams instantly | **RESOLVED** |
